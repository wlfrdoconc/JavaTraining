Deep in the woods, cold and alone,
Hansel and Gretel had lost their way home.
Spotting a cottage of candy and sweet,
They rushed to the doorway for something to eat.
Out came a witch with a wicked old leer,
Trapping the children in hunger and fear;
Bold little Gretel outsmarted the plot,
Pushing the witch to a fiery spot.
Free from the cage and with pockets of gold,
They hurried back home to the safety of old.
