Goldilocks and the Three Bears

Goldilocks went walking one sunny day,
And found a little house along the way.

Three bowls of porridge sat on a table,
She tasted each one as much as she was able.

The first was hot, the second was cold,
The third was perfect, or so we're told.

Three little chairs were waiting there,
She sat on the smallest and broke the chair!

Upstairs she found three cozy beds,
And chose the smallest to rest her head.

The three bears came home and looked around,
And saw that someone had been in town!

Goldilocks woke up and ran away,
She learned to respect others' homes that day.

The End.
